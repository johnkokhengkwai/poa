Understanding the Two Discounts

🛒 Trade Discount

What is it? A price reduction given at the time of purchase (e.g., bulk buying or trade discount).

Accounting Rule: Deducted directly from the List Price on the invoice. NEVER recorded in account books!

⏱️ Cash Discount

What is it? A discount offered to encourage prompt payment within a credit period (e.g., 5% off if paid in 7 days).

Accounting Rule: Calculated on the Net Invoice Price. ALWAYS recorded in account books as Discount Allowed (Expense for Seller) or Discount Received (Income for Buyer).

Golden Calculation Formula

  1. List Price $-$ Trade Discount = Net Invoice Price (Recorded in Sales/Purchases)
  2. Net Invoice Price $-$ Cash Discount = Final Cash Amount Paid/Received
